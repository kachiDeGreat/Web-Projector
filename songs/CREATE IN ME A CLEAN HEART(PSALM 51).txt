Title: CREATE IN ME A CLEAN HEART(PSALM 51)
Artist: CREATE IN ME A CLEAN HEART(PSALM 51)
---
Have mercy upon me, O Yahuah,
According to Your lovingkindness;
According to the multitude of Your tender mercies,
Blot out my transgressions.
Wash me thoroughly from my iniquity,
And cleanse me from my sin.

Create in me a clean heart, O Yahuah,
And renew a steadfast spirit within me.
 Do not cast me away from Your presence,
And do not take Your Ruach from me.
Restore to me the joy of Your salvation,
And uphold me by Your generous Ruach.
Then I will teach transgressors Your ways,
And sinners shall turn back  to You.

 For I acknowledge my transgressions,
And my sin is always before me.
 Against You, You only, have I sinned,
And done this evil in Your sight—
That You may be found just when You speak,
And blameless when You judge.

Create in me a clean heart, O Yahuah
And renew a steadfast spirit within me.
 Do not cast me away from Your presence,
And do not take Your Ruach from me.
Restore to me the joy of Your salvation,
And uphold me by Your generous Ruach.
Then I will teach transgressors Your ways,
And sinners shall be converted to You.

And purify me, purify me
Create in me a clean heart
